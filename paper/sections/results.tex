generators, (RQ2)~channel robustness, and (RQ3)~calibration.  We also present ablation

\subsection{Ablation Studies}

Table~\ref{tab:ablation} isolates the contribution of each AuralGuard component on the
In-the-Wild zero-shot set.  Removing the artifact branch (View~B) increases EER from 4.1\%
to 5.3\%; replacing gated cross-attention with concatenation increases EER to 4.7\%;
switching OC-Softmax to BCE raises EER to 5.9\%; and removing the augmentation curriculum
increases EER to 4.8\%.  All components contribute positively, with the one-class objective
and multi-view fusion providing the largest individual gains.

\subsection{Calibration and Explainability (G3, RQ3)}

AuralGuard produces better-calibrated scores than the BCE-trained baselines.
Temperature scaling reduces ECE from 0.12 to 0.04 on the in-domain set, compared to B5
which requires stronger scaling (ECE 0.15~$\to$~0.07).  We attribute this to the
one-class objective, which naturally produces scores with a clearer interpretation
(distance from the bona-fide center).
