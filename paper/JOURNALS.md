# Scopus-Indexed Target Journals (shortlist)

All Scopus-indexed. Ordered by fit for this manuscript (multi-view SSL
detector, cross-corpus generalization, calibration, 2024–2026 baselines).
Impact figures move yearly — verify on Scopus/journal page before submitting.

## Practical notes

- **Do not resubmit to Information Processing & Management** — the desk
  rejection was scope + currency, and IPM's scope (information retrieval /
  management) is genuinely a poor match for audio anti-spoofing.
- The rejection's two specific complaints are now addressed in the draft:
  current-year references (~30 of 59 entries are 2024–2026) and
  LLM-era baselines (ALLM zero-shot baseline B8 + foundation-model related-work
  subsection + Table VI SOTA comparison).
- Elsevier journals accept "Your Paper Your Way" first submissions, so the
  IEEEtran-formatted PDF can be submitted to CSL / Information Fusion /
  Speech Communication without reformatting; reformat only for revision.
- For TIFS/TASLP, keep IEEEtran as is.
